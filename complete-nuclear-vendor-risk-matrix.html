Data Sensitivity Guidelines

Evaluate the type and classification of data the vendor will access or process:

  • 5 - Critical Nuclear Data/Systems Information: Includes SCADA, plant design, security plans, or safeguards information.
  • 4 - Operations Data: Includes maintenance schedules, fuel management, or personnel records.
  • 3 - Limited Sensitive Data: Administrative or non-critical operational data with restricted access.
  • 2 - Minimal Internal Data: Non-operational data with minimal sensitivity.
  • 1 - Public/Non-Sensitive Data: Publicly available or non-sensitive information.
System Access Guidelines

Measure the level of access the vendor has to nuclear facility systems and networks:

  • 5 - Safety-Critical Systems/OT Network Access: Access to operational technology or safety-critical systems.
  • 4 - Control System Network/ICS Access: Access to industrial control systems or plant networks.
  • 3 - Corporate Network/Limited IT-OT Access: Limited access to IT-OT integrated systems.
  • 2 - Development/Test Environment Only: Access restricted to non-production environments.
  • 1 - No System Access: No access to any systems or networks.
Business Impact Guidelines

Assess the impact on plant operations if vendor services are disrupted:

  • 5 - Reactor Safety/Plant Continuity Function: Disruption could impact reactor safety or plant operations.
  • 4 - Production Systems/Limited Alternatives: Disruption affects production with few alternatives.
  • 3 - Supporting Operations/Back-Office Systems: Disruption impacts supporting functions.
  • 2 - Administrative Function/Multiple Alternatives: Disruption has minimal impact with multiple alternatives.
  • 1 - Minimal Operational Impact/Easily Replaceable: Disruption has negligible impact.
Compliance Requirements Guidelines

Evaluate the regulatory compliance requirements applicable to the vendor:

  • 5 - Nuclear Regulatory Requirements: Subject to NRC, IAEA, or critical DBT regulations.
  • 4 - Safety Systems/NERC CIP Compliance: Must comply with safety or NERC CIP standards.
  • 3 - General Energy Sector Requirements: Subject to general energy sector regulations.
  • 2 - Limited Compliance/Supporting Systems: Minimal compliance requirements for supporting systems.
  • 1 - No Nuclear-Specific Requirements: No specific nuclear regulations apply.
Technical Integration Guidelines

Assess the depth of technical integration with plant systems:

  • 5 - Deep Integration with Safety/Control Systems: Extensive integration with safety or control systems.
  • 4 - Integration with OT/Plant Monitoring Systems: Integration with operational technology or monitoring systems.
  • 3 - Moderate Integration with Supporting Systems: Moderate integration with non-critical systems.
  • 2 - Limited Integration/Isolated Systems: Limited or isolated system integration.
  • 1 - No Technical Integration: No integration with plant systems.
Vendor Maturity Guidelines

Evaluate the vendor's experience and track record in the nuclear industry:

  • 5 - New Vendor/No Nuclear Experience: No prior nuclear industry experience.
  • 4 - Limited Nuclear Industry Experience: Limited experience in nuclear projects.
  • 3 - Established but Limited Nuclear Safety Culture: Established vendor with limited nuclear safety focus.
  • 2 - Nuclear-Qualified/Good Safety Record: Qualified with a good safety record.
  • 1 - Nuclear Industry Leader/Excellent Safety Record: Industry leader with an excellent safety record.
Safety Systems Impact Guidelines

Evaluate the potential impact on safety-critical systems:

  • 5 - Direct Safety Systems Access: Direct access to reactor protection or safety systems.
  • 4 - Secondary Safety Systems Access: Access to secondary safety systems.
  • 3 - Monitoring Systems Access: Access to safety monitoring systems.
  • 2 - Support Systems Access: Access to supporting safety systems.
  • 1 - No Safety Systems Access: No access to safety systems.
Physical Access Level Guidelines

Evaluate the level of physical access to nuclear facility areas:

  • 5 - Vital Area Access: Access to containment or control room areas.
  • 4 - Protected Area Access: Access to power block or protected areas.
  • 3 - Controlled Area Access: Access to technical or controlled areas.
  • 2 - Limited Access: Access to administrative areas only.
  • 1 - No Physical Access: Remote access only.
Radiation Exposure Risk Guidelines

Assess the potential for vendor activities to impact radiation containment or monitoring:

  • 5 - Direct Fuel/Radiation Barrier Interface: Direct interaction with fuel or radiation barriers.
  • 4 - Radiation Monitoring/Containment Systems: Access to radiation monitoring or containment systems.
  • 3 - Secondary Containment/Waste Handling: Involvement in secondary containment or waste handling.
  • 2 - Rad Worker Access/Limited Exposure: Limited radiation exposure as a radiation worker.
  • 1 - No Radiation Area Access: No access to radiation areas.
Supply Chain Security Guidelines

Evaluate the vendor's supply chain integrity controls and verification measures:

  • 5 - Critical Safety Component Supplier: Supplies critical safety components.
  • 4 - OT/Control System Component Supplier: Supplies operational technology or control system components.
  • 3 - Standard Equipment/Parts Supplier: Supplies standard equipment or parts.
  • 2 - Consumables/Non-Critical Parts: Supplies consumables or non-critical parts.
  • 1 - No Physical Components/Services Only: Provides services only, no physical components.
Cyber Defense Posture Guidelines

Evaluate the vendor's cybersecurity defenses and incident response capabilities:

  • 5 - Minimal/Unverified Security Controls: Minimal or unverified cybersecurity measures.
  • 4 - Basic Security/Limited Testing: Basic security with limited testing.
  • 3 - Industry Standard Controls/Regular Testing: Industry-standard controls with regular testing.
  • 2 - Advanced Security Program/Continuous Monitoring: Advanced security with continuous monitoring.
  • 1 - Nuclear-Grade Cybersecurity/Advanced Threat Protection: Nuclear-grade cybersecurity with advanced threat protection.
Nuclear Regulatory Compliance Guidelines

Evaluate the vendor's history with nuclear regulatory requirements and enforcement actions:

  • 5 - Extensive Regulatory Violations: History of significant regulatory violations.
  • 4 - Limited Regulatory Issues: Some regulatory issues but not extensive.
  • 3 - Meets Minimum Requirements: Meets minimum regulatory requirements.
  • 2 - Strong Compliance Record: Strong history of regulatory compliance.
  • 1 - Industry-Leading Compliance: Industry leader in regulatory compliance.
Nuclear Quality Assurance Program Guidelines

Assess alignment with 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B requirements:

  • 5 - No Nuclear QA Program: No quality assurance program specific to nuclear requirements.
  • 4 - Basic QA Program: Basic quality assurance program with limited nuclear focus.
  • 3 - Standard Nuclear QA: Standard nuclear quality assurance program.
  • 2 - Comprehensive Nuclear QA: Comprehensive nuclear quality assurance program.
  • 1 - Industry-Leading Nuclear QA: Industry-leading nuclear quality assurance program.
Nuclear Safety Culture Guidelines

Evaluate the vendor's prioritization of safety over production:

  • 5 - Poor Safety Culture: Little to no emphasis on safety.
  • 4 - Developing Safety Culture: Developing but inconsistent safety culture.
  • 3 - Standard Safety Approach: Standard approach to safety.
  • 2 - Strong Safety Culture: Strong emphasis on safety.
  • 1 - Excellence in Safety Culture: Industry-leading safety culture.
Nuclear Operating Experience Integration Guidelines

Assess how the vendor incorporates industry lessons learned:

  • 5 - No OPEX Integration: No use of operating experience.
  • 4 - Limited OPEX Usage: Limited use of operating experience.
  • 3 - Standard OPEX Program: Standard operating experience program.
  • 2 - Comprehensive OPEX Integration: Comprehensive use of operating experience.
  • 1 - Industry-Leading OPEX Program: Industry-leading operating experience program.
Emergency Preparedness Integration Guidelines

Measure the vendor's integration with site emergency plans:

  • 5 - No Emergency Preparedness: No emergency preparedness measures.
  • 4 - Limited Emergency Awareness: Limited awareness of emergency procedures.
  • 3 - Standard Emergency Procedures: Standard emergency preparedness procedures.
  • 2 - Comprehensive Emergency Integration: Comprehensive integration with emergency plans.
  • 1 - Full Emergency Plan Integration: Full integration with site emergency plans.
Personnel Security Clearance Guidelines

Evaluate the thoroughness of background checks and nuclear-specific vetting:

  • 5 - No Security Vetting: No background checks or vetting.
  • 4 - Basic Background Checks: Basic background checks only.
  • 3 - Standard Nuclear Clearance: Standard nuclear security clearance.
  • 2 - Enhanced Security Vetting: Enhanced vetting procedures.
  • 1 - Top-Tier Security Clearance: Top-tier nuclear security clearance.
Design Basis Knowledge Guidelines

Assess the vendor's understanding of plant licensing basis and design requirements:

  • 5 - No Design Basis Knowledge: No understanding of plant design basis.
  • 4 - Limited Design Understanding: Limited understanding of design requirements.
  • 3 - Standard Design Knowledge: Standard knowledge of plant design basis.
  • 2 - Comprehensive Design Expertise: Comprehensive understanding of design requirements.
  • 1 - Expert Design Authority: Expert-level knowledge of plant design basis.
